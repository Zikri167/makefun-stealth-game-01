Ember Veil — a top-down stealth game. You are Wren, a courier slipping through the lantern-lit Ember Archive at night. Steal every relic, dodge the guards' vision cones, hide in bushes and shadows, and reach the exit unseen.

Three levels, five guards at the hardest, patrols you can learn, a suspicion system that gives you a chance to recover, and a pebble to lure a guard away when patience runs out. Original art, sounds and levels, built from scratch in plain canvas JavaScript.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — move with WASD or arrows, hold Shift to sneak, press Q or click to throw a pebble, P to pause, R to restart.
